Camp Reflections

Looking Back on Camp

A camp is more than just the activities we take part in. It is an opportunity to learn, work together, solve problems and experience new challenges. After each camp, it is important to reflect on what happened, what went well, what could have been better and what we can learn for the future.

This page provides a place to record those reflections and help make future camps safer, more enjoyable and better organised.
