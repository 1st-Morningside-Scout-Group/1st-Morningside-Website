Welcome to 1st Morningside Scout Group

Welcome to 1st Morningside Scout Group, where young people learn, explore, lead and grow through Scouting.

From exciting hikes and camps to practical skills, community service and team challenges, our Scouts are given opportunities to build confidence, make lifelong friendships and take on new adventures.

Whether you're already part of our group or are interested in joining, we’re glad to have you here. Explore our website to learn more about our activities, upcoming events, resources and what makes 1st Morningside a great place to Scout.

Be Prepared. Get Involved. Go Explore.
